## Announcing an Upcoming Landmark Discussion

I am thrilled to announce my involvement in a pivotal upcoming workshop titled **"What is Neuromorphic?"** This event, hosted by Open Neuromorphic (ONM), aims to create a landmark discussion that bridges two fascinating worlds:

1.  **The Technology:** How "neuromorphs" design hardware and software inspired by the brain's architecture.
2.  **The Pedagogy:** How educational theorists develop learning models (like Connectivism) based on how the brain functions and connects information.

In this session, I will have the honor of facilitating the dialogue between two pioneers: **Stephen Downes**, a leader in online learning theory, and **Jens E. Pedersen**, the Chair of Open Neuromorphic.

### My Role: The Bridge

My role will be to act as the crucial link between these two perspectives and the audience. Drawing on my background in education, curriculum design, and coaching, I will:

- **Guide the conversation**, ensuring a fluid and insightful dialogue.
- **Act as a "translator,"** making complex concepts accessible to everyone.
- **Represent the learner's perspective**, focusing on how we can lower barriers for newcomers to this exciting field.

This workshop is more than just a talk; it's an exploration of how we can learn from each other to build a more effective and accessible scientific research community. Stay tuned for more details!
